# Morning AI Scout: 2026-11-12

## Top 5 finds
1. **[Nod by n8n](https://www.producthunt.com/products/n8n-io)** (Free, open source): A personal AI agent that runs on its own "computer" on your Mac or PC, works in Gmail, Slack, Notion and more, and asks before doing anything. You bring your own AI provider (a ChatGPT plan works), so the app is free but model usage can cost.
2. **[MiniMax H3-Image](https://www.producthunt.com/products/minimax)** (Free options): Open-weight 4K image generation and editing in one model. Product Hunt tags it "Free Options" and lists Hailuo AI as a place to try it (exact free limits not stated, check before filming).
3. **[Gemini Decisions API](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/)** (Free plan): Google's new yes/no and scoring endpoint with a confidence score. Free tier in Google AI Studio, but it needs a little code, so it is a niche pick.
4. **[Earlybird](https://www.producthunt.com/products/earlybird-5)** (Free, open source): A tiny 29M-parameter speech-to-text model that is open source. Interesting for creators who want free local captions.
5. **[Scribe v3 by ElevenLabs](https://www.producthunt.com/products/elevenlabs)** (Price not confirmed): Speech-to-text with a claimed 27% fewer errors across 90+ languages, in the dashboard and API. The page does not state pricing or a free tier.
