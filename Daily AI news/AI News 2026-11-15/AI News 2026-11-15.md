# Morning AI Scout: 2026-11-15

## Top 5 finds
1. **[CaptureFlow](https://captureflow.dev/)** (Free plan): Open-source Chrome screen recorder with instant share links, AI transcripts, captions, summaries and chapters. Free for the first 25 recordings (then $5 or $12 per month), or free forever if self-hosted. Launched on Product Hunt on Nov 14.
2. **[ggml v1.0.0](https://github.com/ggml-org/ggml/releases)** (Free): The engine behind many local AI apps (llama.cpp and others) hit v1.0.0 on Nov 13, with faster CUDA, Metal and Vulkan paths. Good background news for the local-AI crowd, but it is a developer library, not an app.
3. **[Antigravity /teamwork](https://research.google/blog/advancing-science-engineering-with-antigravitys-teamwork-feature/)** (Paid, small free part): Google's multi-agent mode in Antigravity (Nov 13 post). Deep Review has 2 free calls a week; one search result says /teamwork preview is for AI Ultra only, so it is mostly a news item.
4. **[Gemini Robotics 2.5](https://www.producthunt.com/products/gemini-robotics-2-5)** (Paid / not public): Google DeepMind's robot model, 30% to 100% higher success than before. Rolling out to robotics partners only, so viewers cannot try it.
5. **[Kokonut UI](https://www.producthunt.com/products/kokonutui)** (Free, open source per its Product Hunt tagline): Open-source UI components for AI-agent apps. Pricing page was not opened, so treat as unconfirmed.
