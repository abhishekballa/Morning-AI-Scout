# Morning AI Scout: 2026-11-10

## Top 5 finds
1. **[Google AI Studio + Google Maps](https://developers.googleblog.com/from-prompt-to-app-build-with-google-maps-in-ai-studio/)** (Free, no credit card): Launched Nov 9. Describe a map app in plain words, like a food trail, travel plan or store locator, and Gemini builds a live, working map app. Free sandbox with 100 calls per day.
2. **[Claude Fable 5.2](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)** (Free plan): Launched Nov 9. Anthropic's best public model: smarter, up to 30% faster, and Anthropic says it's available to all claude.ai users. Free-plan limits aren't published yet.
3. **[Frameboy by World Labs](https://frameboy.worldlabs.ai)** (Free): On Product Hunt Nov 10. Walk through AI-generated 3D worlds right in your browser. Great "wow" B-roll.
4. **[Agent Clan](https://agentclan.app)** (Free app): On Product Hunt Nov 10. Control Claude Code or Codex AI agents as 3D characters with hand gestures, on Mac or Windows. Fun to demo, but viewers need Claude Code or Codex set up.
5. **[Memorias](https://memorias.io)** (Paid, pay-per-use credits): On Product Hunt Nov 10. Talk about your life and AI turns the stories into a memoir book. A sweet gift idea for parents, but not free.

## Today's pick: "Coding లేకుండా 2 Minutes లో మీ సొంత Map App! Google AI Studio Free Tool"
Why this one: It's 100% free with no credit card, needs no coding, and gives a visual result on screen in minutes, which is perfect for beginners. You can make it local, like a "Hyderabad best biryani spots" or "Tirupati trip planner" map, which Telugu viewers will love and English channels won't have. Fable 5.2 is the bigger headline, but its free limits aren't confirmed, while this one viewers can definitely try for free today.

> **Check before filming:** Do one test build at ai.studio/build so you know where the buttons are. The free sandbox gives 100 map calls per day, so don't burn them all while practising.
