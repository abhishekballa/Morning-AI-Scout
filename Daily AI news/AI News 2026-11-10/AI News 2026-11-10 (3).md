## Video outline
1. **What launched**: Fable 5.2 in simple words: faster, smarter, open to all users. One big stat only.
2. **Set up the test**: Three creator prompts: a video script, 5 title ideas, and a Telugu caption. Open claude.ai on screen and show the model menu.
3. **Live demo: side-by-side**: Run each prompt on the old model and on Fable 5.2. Show both answers on screen together.
4. **Score it**: Rate each result on clarity, Telugu quality and speed. Be honest about where the new model isn't better.
5. **Verdict + free limits**: Say what the free plan gives and who should stay free. CTA: comment which prompt you want tested next.
