## Today's pick: Build your own Google Maps app with just a prompt, free (Google AI Studio)
Why this one: Beginners and creators can try it today for free, with no coding and no credit card. It is fresh (launched Nov 9) and visual, so it works well on screen. After watching, viewers can build something real, such as a "best biryani places near me" map or a travel route planner, and show it off.
