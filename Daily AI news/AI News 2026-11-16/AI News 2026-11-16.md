# Morning AI Scout: 2026-11-16

## Top 5 finds
1. **[Tulvo](https://www.tulvo.io/)** (Free plan): An AI workspace where you describe your business and it builds docs, boards and reports for you. The Free plan is "free forever, no credit card" (1,000 blocks, 1 GB, 1 deployed app); Pro is $12 per member per month. Product Hunt launch on Nov 15.
2. **[Rakazo](https://rakazo.com/)** (Free plan, open source): Open-source (Apache-2.0) alternative to Grok Bot. AI "bots" with jobs like Inbox Manager that you message from Slack, iMessage/SMS or mobile apps. Hosted version or self-host. Top of Product Hunt on Nov 15.
3. **[Talyn](https://www.talyn.dev/)** (Free plan): Runs Claude Code and Codex in parallel cloud sandboxes and opens pull requests for you. Free is 10 box-hours a month, but you need your own Claude or ChatGPT plan and GitHub, so it suits developers more.
4. **[Canvas by AgentGrid.io](https://agentgrid.io/)** (Unconfirmed): Visual "agentic design canvas" for working with AI. Pricing not checked, so treat as unconfirmed.
5. **["All AI-generated code should be Open Source"](https://jakub.jankiewicz.org/blog/ai-code-open-source/)** (Free to read): Hacker News front page post with about 1,100 points, posted about 11 hours ago. Good talking point on who owns AI-written code, not a tool.
